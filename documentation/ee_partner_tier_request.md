# Draft: Earth Engine noncommercial Partner tier application (project vista-sindh)

Not submitted. For the author to adapt and submit through the Earth Engine "Manage Tier" page.

**Project.** VISTA-Sindh: village-level environmental stress and recovery forecasting for rural Sindh,
Pakistan, using only free satellite and open data (Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, MODIS, Dynamic World, JRC water,
CHIRPS, ERA5-Land, GHSL, Open Buildings). Outputs are an open village inventory (159,593 settlement clusters
with persistent IDs, 103,659 in the modelling population), 25-year environmental indicator series per
village, and forecasting benchmarks with spatial cross-validation and calibrated uncertainty. Code and data
cards are public (github.com/ArsalanKaleem/VISTA); results are intended for publication and for Sindh
provincial planning after the 2022 floods.

**Why Earth Engine.** The 207,318 village zones (core and resource) require zonal statistics over 10-30 m
imagery for 2000-2025. The pilot (8,245 villages, one year, 12 products) measured 157 EECU-hours; the
measured per-product costs give about 2,300 EECU-hours for the province at seasonal resolution for optical
data and monthly resolution for coarse products, and about 7,000 EECU-hours more for monthly 10 m optical
and SAR series. The Community tier (150 EECU-hours per month) cannot run this within the project's
timeline; the Contributor tier would take three or more months of queue.

**Request.** Partner tier for project vista-sindh for the duration of the extraction (about three months),
expected usage 3,000-10,000 EECU-hours in total, with per-task costs logged
(experiments/ee_operations_snapshot.csv) and published with the results.

**Noncommercial status.** Independent, unfunded research; no commercial use; outputs released under CC BY 4.0.
